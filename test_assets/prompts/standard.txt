# Standard prompts for FastVLM benchmark testing.
# Use --temperature 0 (greedy) for deterministic, comparable results.
# These prompts are used in docs/PERFORMANCE.md benchmarks.

# GENERAL — works on any image
GENERAL=Describe this image in detail.

# DETAIL — tests fine-grained visual perception
DETAIL=Describe exactly what you see in this image. Be specific about colors, objects, and spatial relationships. Do not invent details that are not visible.

# PORTRAIT — for afghan_girl.jpg
PORTRAIT=Describe this portrait. What is the subject wearing? What color are their eyes? What is the background?

# COUNTING — for lunch_skyscraper.jpg
COUNTING=How many people are in this image? What are they doing, and where are they located?

# MINIMAL — for pale_blue_dot.png (hallucination resistance)
MINIMAL=Describe exactly what you see in this image. If the image contains very little visual content, say so clearly.

# SPATIAL — for earthrise.jpg
SPATIAL=What do you see in this image? Describe the colors and how the objects are arranged spatially.
